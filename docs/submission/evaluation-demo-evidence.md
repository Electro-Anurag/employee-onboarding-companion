## Selected business case and differentiation

**Track: end-to-end onboarding coordination with governed AI support.**
Employee/HR/IT/Manager coordinate a single versioned case over pre-joining
and first weeks: typed submissions, explicit HR review, dependencies,
policy version acknowledgement, IT provisioning, meetings, training,
in-app reminders, escalation, exception recovery and closeout.
The distinctive technical contribution is *evidence-governed next steps*:
model outputs are scoped to an authorized case snapshot; a state engine,
not the model, decides whether completion/approval may occur.

Identity systems, external IT services and documents may be **synthetic
or mocked** under the assignment. This is a deliberate scope choice, not a
reason to imply real employee records or access provisioning were integrated.
Never upload real identity documents to this demo.

## Measurable business value — protocol, not invented savings

Use synthetic before/after onboarding journeys that exercise the same 17
tasks, four roles and exception episodes. Capture:
- **Time to identify the right owner:** median seconds from case view to
  current assignee found (manual reference vs companion).
- **Blocked-task detection:** percentage of intentionally seeded blockers
  surfaced and identified before users attempt forbidden completion.
- **Human follow-up work:** number of manual contacts needed to assign,
  clarify and resolve seeded exceptions; record equivalent baseline.
- **Time to legitimate completion:** elapsed active interaction and
  scheduled waits, with any automation excluded from claimed savings when
  the worker is not running.
- **AI factual correctness:** supported-answer precision, unsupported-claim
  refusal, source correspondence, role exclusion and cost per provider call
  on a separately approved, recorded evaluation.

Record number of users/journeys, timestamps, baseline method and raw counts.
Do **not** report percent savings, model accuracy or productivity improvement
until a representative repeatable experiment actually measures them.
